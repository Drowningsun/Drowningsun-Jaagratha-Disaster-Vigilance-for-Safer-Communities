## 🚀 Overview

Jaagratha is a smart disaster vigilance platform designed to help communities, authorities, and rescue teams respond quickly during emergencies such as floods, landslides, fires, and other natural disasters.

The platform provides:

* ⚠️ Real-time emergency alerts
* 📍 Relief center and safe zone information
* 📢 Emergency SMS broadcasting
* 🆘 Incident reporting system
* 🤖 AI-assisted disaster monitoring and prioritization
* 🌐 Community coordination during disasters

Jaagratha aims to reduce response time, improve communication, and make disaster management more efficient and accessible.

---

# ✨ Features

## 🔔 Real-Time Alerts

Receive instant notifications and warnings during emergencies.

## 🆘 Emergency Reporting

Users can quickly report incidents and dangerous situations.

## 📍 Relief & Safe Zone Locator

Find nearby shelters, rescue centers, and emergency support locations.

## 📢 SMS Broadcasting

Emergency updates can be sent through SMS for wider reach during network issues.

## 🤖 AI-Based Assistance

AI helps analyze incoming reports and identify high-priority emergency situations.

## 🌍 Community Safety Platform

Connects citizens, volunteers, and authorities on a single platform.

# 🧠 Role of AI in the Project

Artificial Intelligence is used in Jaagratha to:

* Analyze disaster-related reports and alerts
* Detect possible emergency situations quickly
* Prioritize critical incidents
* Assist authorities in faster decision-making
* Improve response efficiency during disasters

The AI component makes the system smarter, faster, and more reliable during crisis situations.
